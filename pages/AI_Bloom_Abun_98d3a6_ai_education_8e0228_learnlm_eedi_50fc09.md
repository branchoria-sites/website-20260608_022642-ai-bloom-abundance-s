## Introduction

The LearnLM and Eedi maths tutoring trial is one of the clearest early tests of a larger AI bloom question: can advanced AI help people genuinely think better, rather than merely automate answers? In a small but closely supervised UK study, researchers explored whether an AI model designed for learning could support secondary-school mathematics students as effectively as [human tutors]({{ 'human-role/' | relative_url }}). The striking result was not that AI replaced teachers. It was that an AI system operating inside carefully designed constraints, with expert humans overseeing its messages, appeared able to match or even slightly outperform human-only tutoring on some measures of learning transfer. arXiv<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://eedi-labs-67qwnm2lxu4blqi5.webflow.io/news/new-exploratory-research-from-eedi-and-google-deepmind-reveals-human-in-the-loop-ai-tutoring-outperforms-human-only-support" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: eedi-labs-67qwnm2lxu4blqi5.webflow.io">[2eedi-labs-67qwnm2lxu4blqi5.webflow.io]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">eedi-labs-67qwnm2lxu4blqi5.webflow.io</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ction was delivered by LearnLM—a family of models from Google and Google...Read more...</span></span></span>

That matters because mathematics tutoring is a demanding test case. A useful maths tutor must identify misconceptions, ask the right questions, avoid giving away answers too quickly, and help students apply ideas to new problems. But the trial also highlights an equally important lesson. The encouraging results came from a tightly controlled system with expert oversight, not from simply handing students a general-purpose chatbot.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.23633" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: arxiv.org">[arXiv]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">arxiv.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AI tutoring can safely and effectively support studentsDecember 29, 2025 — by LLM Team · 2025 · Cited by 2 — We integrated LearnLM...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 29, 2025</span></span></span> 2eedi.com

## What the UK maths trial actually tested

The study was conducted across five UK secondary schools using Eedi, a mathematics learning platform focused on diagnosing student misconceptions and providing targeted support. Researchers ran an exploratory randomised controlled trial involving 165 students. Pupils were assigned to different forms of support after encountering difficulties with maths questions.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.23633" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: arxiv.org">[arXiv]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">arxiv.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AI tutoring can safely and effectively support studentsDecember 29, 2025 — by LLM Team · 2025 · Cited by 2 — We integrated LearnLM...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 29, 2025</span></span></span><span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://learning-engineering-virtual-institute.org/eedi-showing-how-ai-tutoring-can-deliver-personalized-learning-safely-and-effectively/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: learning-engineering-virtual-institute.org">[Learning]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">learning-engineering-virtual-institute.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Eedi Showing How AI Tutoring Can Deliver Personalized...19 Dec 2025 — Eedi, a UK-based mathematics learning platform that diagnoses stud...</span></span></span>

* Traditional static hints already used on the platform.
* Live human tutors communicating through chat.

LearnLM is Google's family of AI models adapted specifically for educational use. Rather than optimising solely for correct answers, it is designed around learning-science principles such as guided questioning, scaffolding and encouraging student reasoning.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://cloud.google.com/solutions/learnlm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cloud.google.com">[Google Cloud]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cloud.google.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Google CloudLearnLMBuilt in collaboration with experts in education, LearnLM represents our capabilities fine-tuned for learning informed...</span></span></span>

The researchers were not primarily asking whether AI could solve maths problems. Modern language models can often do that already. The question was whether an AI tutor could help students learn mathematics in a way that transferred beyond the immediate exercise. That distinction is crucial. [Education]({{ 'education/' | relative_url }}) systems care far more about durable understanding than about short-term answer production.

## How expert oversight shaped the AI messages

One reason the study attracted attention is that it avoided a common weakness in AI education discussions. Students were not simply chatting freely with a frontier model.

Instead, expert human tutors remained in the loop. LearnLM generated tutoring responses, but human supervisors reviewed and approved messages before they reached students. This arrangement created a hybrid system: AI handled much of the conversational generation while human experts provided quality [control]({{ 'control/' | relative_url }}) and safety oversight.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://eedi-labs-67qwnm2lxu4blqi5.webflow.io/news/new-exploratory-research-from-eedi-and-google-deepmind-reveals-human-in-the-loop-ai-tutoring-outperforms-human-only-support" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: eedi-labs-67qwnm2lxu4blqi5.webflow.io">[eedi-labs-67qwnm2lxu4blqi5.webflow.io]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">eedi-labs-67qwnm2lxu4blqi5.webflow.io</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ction was delivered by LearnLM—a family of models from Google and Google...Read more...</span></span></span><span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251111406294/en/New-Exploratory-Research-From-Eedi-and-Google-DeepMind-Reveals-Human-in-the-Loop-AI-Tutoring-Outperforms-Human-Only-Support" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: businesswire.com">[Business Wire]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">businesswire.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">New Exploratory Research From Eedi and Google...Nov 11, 2025 — Under the supervision of expert human tutors, we tested a model in which...</span></span></span>

## Why novel problem performance mattered so much

The most interesting finding was not that students received help. It was how researchers measured success.

Educational interventions often look impressive when students repeat a problem type they have just practised. The harder question is whether they can apply the underlying concept somewhere new.

The Eedi-LearnLM research therefore examined performance on subsequent and unfamiliar problems. Students who received supervised LearnLM tutoring were reported to be around 5.5 percentage points more likely to solve novel problems successfully than students who worked with human tutors alone. The reported success rates were approximately 66.2% for supervised LearnLM tutoring compared with 60.7% for human tutoring. ## Why the tutoring style mattered more than the model

A common misunderstanding is that the positive results prove increasingly powerful AI models automatically produce better learning outcomes.

The study points in a different direction. LearnLM's design emphasised pedagogical behaviour. ## What the trial did not prove

The findings are promising, but the study has important limits.

First, it was relatively small. The trial involved 165 students across five schools. ## Why this case matters for cognitive empowerment

Even with those caveats, the trial represents something unusual: evidence that AI may be able to support high-quality reasoning rather than merely automate information retrieval.

Historically, individual tutoring has been among the most effective educational interventions available, but it remains expensive and difficult to scale. If systems like Eedi and LearnLM can preserve meaningful parts of tutoring quality while dramatically lowering costs, they could expand access to forms of personalised learning previously available mainly to wealthier families or better-resourced schools.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.23633" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: arxiv.org">[arXiv]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">arxiv.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AI tutoring can safely and effectively support studentsDecember 29, 2025 — by LLM Team · 2025 · Cited by 2 — We integrated LearnLM...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 29, 2025</span></span></span>

This connects directly to the larger AI bloom vision.

A flourishing future is not only about producing more goods and services. It also depends on increasing humanity's collective ability to understand, discover, reason and create. Scientific progress, better governance, technological innovation and long-term civilisational [resilience]({{ 'resilience/' | relative_url }}) all rely on human cognitive capacity.

The strongest version of the optimistic case is therefore not "AI learns for us". It is "AI helps more people learn". The Eedi-LearnLM experiment is notable because it tested that possibility in a concrete educational setting rather than treating it as a distant aspiration.

    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;

    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });

      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }

  }

      });

      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });

      });

    }

      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
